# 0.1.1 — 2026-09-26

First published release: six read-only `uklaw_*` tools over legislation.gov.uk (The National Archives), keyless, with no resources or prompts. Ships the work tracked in [#2](https://github.com/cyanheads/uk-legislation-mcp-server/issues/2)–[#10](https://github.com/cyanheads/uk-legislation-mcp-server/issues/10) and [#12](https://github.com/cyanheads/uk-legislation-mcp-server/issues/12)–[#14](https://github.com/cyanheads/uk-legislation-mcp-server/issues/14), and the `read_back_to` part of [#11](https://github.com/cyanheads/uk-legislation-mcp-server/issues/11).

## Added

- **`uklaw_search_legislation`** — full-text or title search, or a type/year listing, filtered by `types`, `year` or `year_from`/`year_to`, `extent` with `extent_match`, and `as_of`; `limit` 1–50, `page` 1–10,000. Title searches and listings carry type and year facets.
- **`uklaw_get_document`** — an item or provision as `current`, `enacted` or dated text (`version`), in English or Welsh (`language`), with editorial status, annotations, up to 20 unapplied effects, and the versions available. Oversized reads return an `outline`, filtered by `match_text` and paged by `outline_offset`.
- **`uklaw_get_amendments`** — effects made to (`direction: affected`) or by (`affecting`) an item, a type, or a type and year, filtered by `counterpart`, `status` and `provision`; `limit` 1–100, default 20, continued with `cursor`. A `provision` filter scans up to 1,500 effects per call.
- **`uklaw_track_changes`** — the Publication Log for a window of up to 31 days, filtered by `content_type`, `direction`, `category`, `item`, `new_only` and `event`. A full item path reads that item's own log and reports `read_back_to`; other scopes walk the window day by day, up to today's UK date.
- **`uklaw_lookup_citation`** — resolves a numbered citation, short title or legislation.gov.uk URI, with an optional provision, to its item path, and checks the provision exists in the current version. A miss or an ambiguous title returns `found: false` with `guidance` or up to 20 `candidates`.
- **`uklaw_list_reference`** — offline tables for 12 topics: type codes, type groups, extents, versions, document status, annotation types, effect fields, provision paths, citation formats, Publication Log vocabulary, coverage, and attribution.
- **Configuration** — all optional: `UK_LEGISLATION_CONTACT` (appended to the `User-Agent`), `UK_LEGISLATION_MIN_REQUEST_GAP_MS` (default 1000, minimum 250), and `UK_LEGISLATION_CACHE_MAX_MB` (default 64, range 8–1024).
- **legislation.gov.uk client** — requests are paced process-wide (raised to a robots.txt crawl delay for this user agent, up to 60 s), cached with `If-Modified-Since` revalidation, and capped at 24 MiB per body; a 403 or 429 fails `upstream_refused` with `retryAfter`.
- **Attribution** — every response that returns legislation carries the Open Government Licence line, plus the EU or Westlaw credit where its content needs one.
- **Distribution** — npm package, MCP Registry entry (`server.json`, published with `bun run publish-mcp`), `.mcpb` bundle for Claude Desktop, multi-arch Docker image on GHCR, and Claude Code and Codex plugin manifests.
