# 0.2.2 — 2026-11-25

## Added

- **`provision.enclosing`** on `uklaw_get_document` provision reads: the elements around the provision (Part, Chapter, cross-heading, Schedule, and a sub-provision's section), outermost first, each with its `path`, `label` and `heading` (#30).
- **`item.made_date`, `item.laid`, `item.coming_into_force`, `associated_documents` and `links.notes`** on `uklaw_get_document`: the item's made, laid and in-force dates, and links to its explanatory notes or memorandum and the other documents published with it (#31).
- **`UK_LEGISLATION_CLAMP_LIMITS`** (default `true`): `false` rejects a `limit` above the cap and advertises `maximum` at each cap (#33).

## Changed

- **`limit` caps**: `uklaw_search_legislation` 50 → 1,000 and `uklaw_get_amendments` 100 → 500; `uklaw_track_changes` stays at 80 (#32).
- **A `limit` above the cap** is served at the cap with a notice naming both values, instead of rejected; each clamped call adds one log line and one `uklaw.limit.clamped` count, and the schemas advertise no `maximum` (#33).
- **Citation and provision parsing** scan their input in linear time.

## Fixed

- **Citation shorthand reads letter-prefixed numbers** (`Sch. B1 para. 14`, `s. A6`) on `uklaw_get_document`, `uklaw_get_amendments` and `uklaw_lookup_citation`, and a leading Schedule splits before "to" (`Schedule 2 to the Data Protection Act 2018` → `ukpga/2018/12`, `schedule/2`) (#29).
- **A comma between shorthand components** is read (`Data Protection Act 2018, Sch. 2, para. 3`).
- **Roman values with a letter suffix** are read (`Pt XA`, `chapter/VIIA`).
- **Effect ranges with letter-prefixed endpoints** cover their interior (`s. A29-A32` reaches `section/A30`).
- **`links.pdf`** follows the served language: an English read of a Welsh Act returns the English PDF.
- **Outline entries for cross-headings that carry only a number** get that number as their heading.
- **Rendered text** labels a `Match="false"` element *(prospective — …)* only when upstream marks it `Status="Prospective"`; repealed and other not-in-force elements get their own labels.
