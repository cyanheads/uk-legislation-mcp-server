# 0.2.2 — 2026-11-25

## Added

- **`provision.enclosing`** on `uklaw_get_document` provision reads: the elements around the provision (Part, Chapter, cross-heading, Schedule, and a sub-provision's section), outermost first, each with its `path`, `label` and `heading` ([#30](https://github.com/cyanheads/uk-legislation-mcp-server/issues/30)).
- **`item.made_date`, `item.laid`, `item.coming_into_force`, `associated_documents` and `links.notes`** on `uklaw_get_document`: the item's made, laid and in-force dates, and links to its explanatory notes or memorandum and the other documents published with it ([#31](https://github.com/cyanheads/uk-legislation-mcp-server/issues/31)).
- **`UK_LEGISLATION_CLAMP_LIMITS`** (default `true`): `false` rejects a `limit` above the cap and advertises `maximum` at each cap ([#33](https://github.com/cyanheads/uk-legislation-mcp-server/issues/33)).

## Changed

- **`limit` caps**: `uklaw_search_legislation` 50 → 1,000 and `uklaw_get_amendments` 100 → 500; `uklaw_track_changes` stays at 80 ([#32](https://github.com/cyanheads/uk-legislation-mcp-server/issues/32)).
- **A `limit` above the cap** is served at the cap with a notice naming both values, instead of rejected; each clamped call adds one log line and one `uklaw.limit.clamped` count, and the schemas advertise no `maximum` ([#33](https://github.com/cyanheads/uk-legislation-mcp-server/issues/33)).
- **Citation and provision parsing** scan their input in linear time.
- **Tooling and skills resynced** to `@cyanheads/mcp-ts-core` 0.13.22: `scripts/`, `framework-skills/`, `changelog/template.md`, `.mcpbignore`, and the `MCP_JWT_EXPECTED_AUDIENCE` and `MCP_JWT_EXPECTED_ISSUER` lines in `.env.example`; the `.mcpb` also leaves out each dependency's root `AGENTS.md` and `CLAUDE.md`.

## Fixed

- **Citation shorthand reads letter-prefixed numbers** (`Sch. B1 para. 14`, `s. A6`) on `uklaw_get_document`, `uklaw_get_amendments` and `uklaw_lookup_citation`, and a leading Schedule splits before "to" (`Schedule 2 to the Data Protection Act 2018` → `ukpga/2018/12`, `schedule/2`) ([#29](https://github.com/cyanheads/uk-legislation-mcp-server/issues/29)).
- **A comma between shorthand components** is read (`Data Protection Act 2018, Sch. 2, para. 3`).
- **Roman values with a letter suffix** are read in shorthand (`Pt XA` → `part/XA`, `Ch. VIIA` → `chapter/VIIA`).
- **Effect ranges with letter-prefixed endpoints** cover their interior (`s. A29-A32` reaches `section/A30`).
- **`links.pdf`** follows the served language: an English read of a Welsh Act returns the English PDF.
- **Cross-headings that carry only a number** get that number as their heading, in `provision.heading` ([#30](https://github.com/cyanheads/uk-legislation-mcp-server/issues/30)) and in outline entries.
- **Rendered text** labels a `Match="false"` element *(prospective — …)* only when upstream marks it `Status="Prospective"`, and *(not in force at this version)* when it carries neither that nor `Status="Repealed"`.
