# 0.2.1 — 2026-10-29

## Changed

- **`uklaw_get_document` provision reads** list the effects on the provisions enclosing the provision and, on full text, those inside it, from the item's unapplied changes feed (#1). Pre-1963 Acts are checked through the calendar-year feed. A notice says when the check could not run, and `editorial.caveat` then says those effects were not checked. Item-level routes to `uklaw_get_amendments` name the calendar-year path.
- **`item.path`** reports the path legislation.gov.uk served for a redirected input, with a notice (#20).
- **Shared calendar path** — a calendar path two Acts share (`ukpga/1955/19`) fails ValidationError `ambiguous_item` with `data.candidates`, where it was ServiceUnavailable (#20).
- **`uklaw_get_amendments`** labels a filtered read's counts "Matched this call" and "item total", and the scan line says how much of the item's feed this call scanned (#21).
- **`uklaw_track_changes` cursor** gains optional `h` (item-log page-search bound) and `e` (events-returned mark); earlier cursors still decode (#11, #23).

## Fixed

- **Redirected item paths** — `ukpga/1925/20` (served at `ukpga/Geo5/15-16/20`) and a Welsh SI addressed as `uksi` (served at `wsi`) lose their heading, outline, validity dates and effects; they are now read at the served path (#20).
- **Dated read before the earliest revised version** gets a notice naming the version served (#18).
- **`match_text`** in the zero-match notice is escaped, so a term such as `zz*q*` reads back as written in `content[]` (#19).
- **Dated read answered with the original text** — a 307 to the original text gets the original-text notice and caveat (#24). A date before the made date of an original-only item (`wsi/2024/1337` at 2020-01-01) is now answered not-found; it returned made text the item did not have on that date.
- **Citation-prefix URIs** — effects matched only through a citation prefix (`schedule/10` for "Sch. 10 para. 6(1)(c)") are no longer returned for other provisions under it (#25).
- **`uklaw_track_changes` item log** finds an old window by a galloping page search instead of reading every page between today and the window (#11).
- **`uklaw_track_changes` empty continuation** — a call that returns no events after earlier calls returned them says the window has no further events, without filter advice; a window that returned none keeps the quiet-window notice (#23).
- **PDF-only original Act** — a dated read returns its original text (`ukla/1955/1` at 2000-01-01 was `version_not_found`) (#26).
- **Outlined container caveat** counts the effects `unapplied_effects` lists and points `uklaw_get_amendments` at the rest (#27).
